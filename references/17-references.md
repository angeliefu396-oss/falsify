# 十七、研究依据与适用限制

预测校准部分参考：

- Mellers, B. A., et al. (2014). *Psychological Strategies for Winning a Geopolitical Forecasting Tournament*. Psychological Science, 25(5), 1106–1115. <https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797614524255>
- Mellers, B. A., et al. (2015). *The Psychology of Intelligence Analysis: Drivers of Prediction Accuracy in World Politics*. Journal of Experimental Psychology: Applied, 21(1), 1–14. <https://doi.org/10.1037/xap0000040>
- Gneiting, T., & Raftery, A. E. (2007). *Strictly Proper Scoring Rules, Prediction, and Estimation*. JASA, 102(477), 359–378. <https://doi.org/10.1198/016214506000001437>

这些研究支持概率训练、参考类别、反证、协作、更新和适当评分可以改善特定预测任务表现；它们不证明单个用户、所有领域或任意数量样本都能获得同样效果，也不证明预测准确率等同于完整决策质量。
